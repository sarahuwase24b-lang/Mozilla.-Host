About Our Driving Academy

Uwase Sarah Driving Academy is a professional driving school located in Kigali, Rwanda. Our mission is to help learners become safe, skilled, and confident drivers through quality training and road safety education.

We provide both theoretical and practical driving lessons for beginners and experienced drivers. Our instructors are committed to helping students succeed in their driving journey.

Our Mission

To provide professional driving education and promote road safety in Rwanda through high-quality training.

Our Vision

To become one of the leading driving academies in Rwanda known for safety, excellence, and student success.
